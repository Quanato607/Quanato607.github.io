Visiting Research Student @ University of Michigan · Fall 2027 Applicant

Shenghao Zhu

Medical Image Analysis · Longitudinal Tumor World Models · Multi-Turn Clinical Consultation

I am a Computer Science and Technology graduate of Hangzhou Dianzi University (B.Eng., July 2026; GPA 4.28/5.0) and currently a Visiting Research Student with Prof. Liyue Shen at the University of Michigan. Previously, I interned as a Medical AI Researcher with Dr. Le Lu at Ant Group, following research internships at Westlake University and Tsinghua University.

My research bridges two complementary fronts: (1) medical image-assisted diagnosis and longitudinal patient world models for missing-modality 3D MRI and forecasting subsequent tumor progression (PD and BN20 scores), and (2) multi-turn clinical consultation via fact-grounded patient simulation (XPatient) and evidence-attributed reinforcement learning (IDEA-RL). Going forward, I aim to unify quantitative image analysis with longitudinal clinical questioning so diagnostic agents know both what evidence to inspect and when to ask.
